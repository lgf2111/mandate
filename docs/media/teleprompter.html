Mandate: spending guardrails for AI shopping agents, settled with PayPal.
02 · the gap
Today an agent with a card can spend anywhere the card clears. The controls live at the bank.
03 · who it is for
This is for someone delegating a bounded purchase: the week's groceries, school supplies, a replacement part.
04 · the product
Say the goal once. Approve the limits the app drafted. Then let it buy.
05 · PayPal
PayPal is the money path: OAuth, Orders two, and a capture that only happens after approval.
06 · the model
The model picks the item and writes the rationale. It cannot set a price, invent a SKU, or authorise anything.
07 · the boundary
One pure function decides, and checkout runs it again, so calling the endpoint directly cannot skip a rule.
08 · the refusal
Here the cart is a hundred and fifteen dollars, above the hundred dollar approval line. Nothing moves.
09 · settled
Approve, and PayPal settles it. The ledger keeps the row, and the budget drops to eighty five.
10 · proof
Fifty four tests, CI green, and a real sandbox capture recorded with its capture id.
11 · why different
A capped card cannot say the cart is ninety four dollars but only eighty five remains. A per cart policy can.
12 · close
